Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the Pharmplained website and the Pharmplained publishing app ("the app"), which Pharmplained uses only to publish its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to Pharmplained's own accounts. With the account owner's permission it uses the YouTube Data API (to upload videos), the Instagram Platform API and the Facebook Pages API (to publish Reels and post a sources comment). It does not read, collect, store or share data about viewers or any other users.

Data stored

The app stores only the access tokens needed to publish to Pharmplained's own accounts, kept privately on the owner's computer, and a log of what was posted (date, video name, post link). Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Meta platforms

Use of Instagram and Facebook is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. Access can be removed in Facebook or Instagram settings under Apps and websites.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Children

Pharmplained content is not directed to children under 13 and the app collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: send a message to @pharmplained on Instagram.
